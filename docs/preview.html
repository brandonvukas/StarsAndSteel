The Concept

A World That Keeps Spinning

Imagine a grand strategy game where the world doesn't pause when you log off. Your economy produces, your air wings patrol, and your rivals scheme — all in real time. You drop in for ten minutes before lunch, give some orders, and check back after dinner to see how it played out.

One Living World Map

Every player shares the same Earth, the same economy, the same clock. A nation rises in Europe while another falls in the Pacific — and you're all watching the same news feed about it.

Friends + AI Rivals

Bring four or five friends. The remaining great powers are filled by AI rivals — each with a personality, grudges, and habits. Some will trade with you. Some absolutely will not.

Combined Arms Strategy

Tanks die to attack helos. Helos die to AA. AA dies to bombers. Bombers die to fighters. You are constantly playing rock-paper-scissors at theater scale.

Ten-Minute Sessions

Designed to fit around real life. Check in, give orders, log off. The game doesn't demand your evening — it rewards your attention when you have it to give.

Cable News Feed

Wake up, open the in-game news app, see what the world did while you slept. Drone strikes in the Aegean. Bread protests in Detroit. Your alliance maybe still intact.

Diplomacy Has Teeth

Combat is the visible layer. The real game is whether the player on your border believes your treaty. Treachery has consequences. Nuclear deterrence shapes every conversation.

A Day In The Life

Tuesday, 7:42 AM

What it actually feels like to play this game during a normal week.

You're brushing your teeth and your phone buzzes.

Breaking The notification reads: "DRONE SWARM STRIKES YOUR EASTERN AIRBASE — HEAVY CASUALTIES." Brandon's air wing hit you while you were asleep. You open the app. Your fighter squadron at Raleigh is wiped. He's still got Combat Drones in the air.

Two orders. Scramble your reserve fighters from Atlanta to intercept his next wave. Queue an AA Battery at the airbase to plug the hole. Twelve ticks until it builds. That's twelve real hours.

In the alliance chat, Mike asks if anyone's seen Sarah today. The Industrialist AI to your south just proposed a non-aggression pact — probably because she sees you're bleeding. Tempting. Risky. You sign it anyway.

You log off. You go to work.

At lunch you check back. Your interceptors splashed his second wave. The news ticker reads: "PROTESTS IN BRANDON'S CAPITAL — MORALE COLLAPSING AFTER FAILED RAID." You smile. You eat your sandwich.

When We'll Build It

The Roadmap

Built in phases so you can play it as soon as the core works, then watch it get better. Each phase ends with a real, demoable build you can put in front of the friend group.

PHASE 1 The Core Game ~4 weekends

Everything you need to actually play. World map, modern economy, five units (Mech Infantry, MBT, AA, Combat Drone, Multirole Fighter), real-time tick, combined-arms combat, the cable news feed, one AI rival (the Hawk). End of this phase: you and your friends can play a complete game.

PHASE 2 Diplomacy & Air Power ~4 weekends

Treaties, alliances, chat channels, research tree. Add Attack Helicopters, Strategic Bombers, Recon Drones, Special Forces. Naval comes online (frigates, destroyers, carriers). More AI personalities. Real strategic depth.

PHASE 3 Strategic Warfare ~4 weekends

Stealth bombers, cruise & ballistic missiles, cyber warfare cells, special ops, doctrines, wonders, theater commanders, random world events. Optional nukes. Late-game tension feels distinct from early game.

PHASE 4 Polish & Sweetener open-ended

Global commodities market, replay viewer, custom flags, weather, achievements, AI intelligence advisor. The fun-stretch list — none required, all great.
